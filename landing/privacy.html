Privacy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Before publishing: replace every [[PLACEHOLDER]] on this page. This is a starting draft written for a small software seller in the EU, not legal advice — have it checked if you are unsure.

The Aurora software collects nothing and contacts no server of ours, ever. This website has no analytics and sets no cookies. If you buy a licence, our payment provider handles your details, and we see your name, email and country — enough to issue the key and keep the invoice.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Aurora, operating from Spain. Payments and billing data are handled by [[PAYMENT PROVIDER]] as merchant of record, who is the seller and the controller for everything to do with the transaction. Contact: hello@aurora-media.shop.

The Aurora software

Aurora runs on hardware you control. It has no telemetry, no crash reporting, no update ping and no account system of ours. It never sends us your library, your viewing history or anything else, because there is nowhere for it to send them.

Licence keys are verified offline. A key is a signed token; your server checks the signature with a public key built into the binary. Activating Aurora Plus makes no network request and works with the machine disconnected from the internet.

Aurora does reach third parties that you configure, using your credentials, directly from your server: TMDB for artwork and descriptions, AniList for anime, and optionally TheTVDB, OpenSubtitles and any source adapters you add. Those requests never pass through us. Each of those services has its own privacy policy and your keys stay in your own database.

Buying a licence

Payments are handled by [[PAYMENT PROVIDER]], who acts as merchant of record. We never see your card details — they never reach our systems. From the purchase we receive your name, email address, country and the order reference.

  • Why: to deliver the licence key you bought (performance of a contract) and to keep the invoice (legal obligation).
  • How long: the licence record for as long as the licence is valid; invoices for 6 years, as Spanish accounting rules require.
  • Who else sees it: the payment provider, and nobody else. Your data is not sold, rented or shared for advertising, and it is not used to train anything.

If you email us for support, we keep that thread until the matter is closed and for a reasonable period afterwards.

Transfers outside the EEA

Our hosting and payment providers may process data outside the European Economic Area under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of processing, portability, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to hello@aurora-media.shop and we will answer within one month. If you are not satisfied you can complain to your national data-protection authority (the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, AEPD).

Deleting your data does not switch off a licence key you already hold: keys are verified offline and your copy keeps working.

Children

Aurora is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes affecting existing customers will be sent to the email address on the order.
