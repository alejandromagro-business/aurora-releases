Thank you.

Your licence key is on its way by email. Everything below is what to do with it — and what to do if it does not turn up.

  1. 1
    Check your email The key arrives within a few minutes. If it is not there, look in spam before anything else — it is a message full of random characters from a sender you have never had mail from, which is exactly what a spam filter is built to catch.
  2. 2
    Open Dashboard → Aurora Plus Paste the key in. Everything unlocks immediately: no restart, and no internet connection needed, because your own server checks the signature itself.
  3. 3
    Keep the email It is the only copy of your key. If you lose it, write to us and we will send it again — but a saved email is faster than we are.

Nothing arrived?

Write to hola@aurora-media.shop with the email address you paid with. You will get a reply within two working days, and a key resent the same day it is found.
