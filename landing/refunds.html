Refunds

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Email us within 14 days and you get your money back. No form, no reason needed, no questions about why. The whole product runs on your own hardware, so there is nothing for us to take away from you if you change your mind.

Our policy

Write to [[CONTACT EMAIL]] from the address you bought with, within 14 days of the purchase, and we refund it in full. You do not have to justify it. We aim to process refunds within 5 working days; the money reaches you by the same method you paid with, and how long your bank takes after that is up to them.

Try the free core first if you can — it is the same application, and it costs nothing to find out whether Aurora runs well on your hardware before paying for anything.

Your legal right of withdrawal

As a consumer in the EU you normally have 14 days to withdraw from a distance purchase without giving a reason. For digital content delivered immediately, that right ends once delivery has begun, if you asked for it to start straight away and acknowledged losing the right of withdrawal — which is what the checkbox at checkout is for.

We do not rely on that exception. We refund within 14 days on request even though a licence key is delivered immediately, so you keep the practical benefit either way.

Monthly plans

Cancel whenever you want, from your account with [[PAYMENT PROVIDER]] or by emailing us. Cancelling stops the next charge; Plus stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. We do not pro-rate part-months, and we do not charge a cancellation fee.

If a renewal caught you by surprise and you had not used Aurora in that period, tell us and we will refund it.

If the key does not arrive

That is not a refund case, it is a delivery problem: write to [[CONTACT EMAIL]] with your order reference and we will reissue the key. If we cannot get a working key to you, you get a full refund regardless of how long ago you bought it.

What we cannot refund

  • Purchases made through someone else's reseller or a key bought from a third party — ask them.
  • Charges older than 14 days, other than the delivery and renewal cases above. Ask anyway if something went wrong; this policy is a floor, not a ceiling.

How to ask

One email to [[CONTACT EMAIL]] with your order reference. That is the entire process. If you prefer the formal route, you may also use the model withdrawal form in Annex I(B) of Directive 2011/83/EU, but you do not need it.
